Andréa Doreste - Software Engineer

Hi! My name is Andréa! I'm a Brazilian Software Engineer with experience in both industry and academic environments, passionate about the software development process — especially the phases that often go unseen, such as specification (understanding what the software needs to do) and verification and testing (ensuring it is done correctly).

Throughout my career, I have gravitated toward challenging problems where the right solution is still being discovered. At the same time, I have also worked on projects where I could apply my knowledge to deliver concrete, short-term value to stakeholders and development teams alike.

I'm currently finishing my PhD in the TAU (Testing Automated) group at the Università della Svizzera italiana (USI), Switzerland, where my research focuses on testing Autonomous Driving Systems using Reinforcement Learning.

Previously, I worked as a Business and Quality Analyst at Radix Engineering and Software (Brazil), where I had the opportunity to collaborate directly with stakeholders from a US-based Oil & Gas company and with the development team — acting as an interface across specification, design, testing, and validation activities.

Beyond driving systems, I find it remarkable when Software Engineering intersects with real-life needs and diverse domains such as botany, microbiology, and beyond.
